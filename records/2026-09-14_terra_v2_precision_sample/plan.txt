The task combines visual cues such as pose, framing, and expression with twelve sparse binary annotations, while the tiny fixture makes a compact model and strict holdout discipline more useful than a large architecture. I use a small depthwise-separable CNN with adaptive pooling plus a normalized tabular MLP, so the two modalities can contribute independently before a learned fusion head predicts the continuous Pawpularity score. The tabular scaler is fitted after a fixed train/validation split, and image preprocessing is shared exactly by validation and test inference to avoid leakage and inference skew. Two epochs, modest augmentation on training images, AdamW, and dropout provide a short from-scratch learning path without claiming that the synthetic fixture measures quality. The V100 path uses configurable FP16 AMP with GradScaler and FP32 optimizer state; CPU or unavailable CUDA cleanly falls back to FP32. The hypothesis is supported only if holdout RMSE improves across the short run and the trained forward pass produces bounded predictions for every test Id; an unstable or poor holdout RMSE would motivate revisiting capacity or regularization rather than claiming a measured speedup.
